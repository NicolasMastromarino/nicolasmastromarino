## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4, next-intl for bilingual (EN/ES) routing. User specified the deploy/data targets: Vercel (hosting) + Supabase (Postgres, free tier) for blog posts and contact form submissions.

## Users

Small business owners and operators who need CRM setup, migration, or ongoing hands-on management, plus marketing/sales-ops automation — primarily:
- Home services / field service businesses (dispatch, invoicing, ticketing needs)
- Marketing/creative agencies who subcontract CRM/automation implementation work
- Coaches, course creators, and personal-brand builders selling via funnels/CRM

Global/remote, English or Spanish speaking. They typically discover the site, review services/case studies, then book a call or submit the contact form — pricing is never published, every engagement is custom-quoted.

## Product Purpose

A freelance consulting site for Nicolás Mastromarino (operating under his own name) to win CRM implementation/management and marketing-automation engagements. Success = qualified leads via the contact form or booked calls, positioning him for a path toward full-time freelance work.

## Positioning

A hands-on, GoHighLevel-led but platform-agnostic automation practitioner who has actually run the day-to-day CRM operations (sales tracking, invoicing, ticketing, reporting, onboarding) inside real businesses — not just implemented a CRM and left. That distinguishes him from agencies that only do setup/strategy and hand off.

## Operating Context

Remote/global client engagements conducted via calls (booked through a calendar link) and async communication (contact form, email). No published pricing — every service ends in a "request a quote" style CTA. Bilingual: the site serves both English and Spanish audiences under `/en` and `/es`.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility through specificity — name the real tools and workflow types (GoHighLevel, Zapier, Kickserv, Airtable, Stripe), not generic automation buzzwords.
2. Practitioner over agency — emphasize hands-on day-to-day CRM/ops work, not just strategy or one-time setup.
3. Low-friction contact — every page leads toward booking a call or reaching out; no pricing friction, no gated content.
4. Global-remote-first and bilingual (EN/ES) by design, not an afterthought.
5. Honest proof — case studies are clearly representative/sanitized, never presented as verbatim named-client data or fabricated testimonials.
